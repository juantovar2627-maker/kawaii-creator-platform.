# Original Mimi demo assets

`mimi-hero.png` and `mimi-gallery.png` are original generated illustrations
created for this Phase 1 prototype. Depicted people are fictional adult creators;
they do not represent accounts or real community members. The gallery is a
four-panel illustration displayed with proportional CSS crops.

`video-poster.svg`, `../media/color-study.mp4` and its descriptive caption track
are original demo assets. The video is a four-second silent pink/lavender color
study generated with FFmpeg. No third-party creator photos or videos are used.
